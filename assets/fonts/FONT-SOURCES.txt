Chinese name font: a three-character subset of LXGW WenKai Lite Regular v1.522.
Source: https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai-Lite
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see wenkai-OFL.txt.

Original copyright and license records are retained inside the font.

English font: Jost, variable weight 100-900, upright and italic.
Source: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/jost
Original project: https://github.com/indestructible-type/Jost
License: SIL Open Font License 1.1; see jost-OFL.txt.
The official TTF files are converted to WOFF2 with fontTools, retaining all
glyphs, variation axes, font names, copyright records and license metadata.
These files are served by the website without an external font service.

English body uses Jost 370; emphasis and entry titles use Jost 500.
Section headings use Jost 550.
